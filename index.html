I am the founder of Softscotch, a digital marketing agency that has spent more than nine years helping businesses across 40 industries get found, trusted and chosen. Our work covers SEO, AI search visibility, Google and Meta ads, email, SMS and WhatsApp, automation and websites.

Why an agency started building products

Agency life has a rhythm. Each client feels different, yet the same problems arrive again and again. Audits with no priorities. Founders who never find time to post. Small brands that cannot reach creators. For years we solved these by hand, one account at a time. Eventually the repetition became the signal: if ten clients had a problem, thousands of businesses without an agency had it too.

What came out of that

PostInstantly was built for the founder staring at a blank LinkedIn post. It learns your voice and turns three short answers into a draft. BrandsForCreators was built for the small brand that wants genuine creator content without weeks of cold messages. DarnItSEO was built for the team holding an audit with hundreds of issues and no idea where to begin.

Three smaller tools followed. Marketing Newsfeed came from our own team learning about platform changes too late. VerifiedWidget came from local clients who kept asking to show their Google reviews. amlookingfor came from founders asking where they should list their product at launch.

How I think about it

The agency keeps us close to real problems, and the products let us solve the most common ones once, for everyone. Client work tells us what to build next, and the products make the client work better.
